Phases 2–4 — Three Parallel Intelligence Streams
Stream A — Semantic

🎯 Object Detection

Two-stage cascade scores each scene by the saliency of detected objects, with Kalman tracking and adaptive persistence.

O_i ∈ [0,1] B1 Hua 2025 • B3 Shah 2026 • B4 Yang 2024
Stage 1: YOLOv8-S
Always-on. 28.6 GFLOPs/frame. Anchor-free, decoupled head.
↑ Fixes B1: single heavy model on all frames
Stage 2: YOLOv9-E (gated)
Fires only when Stage 1 conf ≥ 0.60. ~20% of frames trigger it.
∼69.5% FLOP savings (B3 RR formula)
Kalman Tracker
Adaptive τ = τ_sec × fps. At 25fps: 125 frames (not fixed 75).
↑ Fixes B3: fixed τ=75 assumes 15fps
Decomposed Salience
O_i = 0.5·conf + 0.3·stability + 0.2·persistence
↑ Fixes B2: raw confidence only
SaIS (training-only)
SaIS = IoU + 0.5·shape_score. Zero inference cost.
↑ B4 Yang 2024 SaLA
Stream B — Affective

😊 Facial Emotion Recognition

CLCM backbone + Grad-CAM intensity + binary expert CNNs + LSTM temporal aggregation.

E_i ∈ [-1,1] A2 Abbas 2025 • A3 Punuri 2024 • A4 Salman 2025 • A5 Kosta 2023
CLCM Backbone (~2.4M params)
Custom Lightweight CNN (primary paper A2). WeightedFERLoss with inverse-sqrt-freq class weights.
↑ Fixes A2: class imbalance on CK+ lab data
Grad-CAM
Per-pixel heatmap for every face. Works on ALL frames, not just correct ones.
↑ Fixes A3 LRP: fails on misclassified frames
Binary Expert CNNs (Disgust/Fear)
2 experts triggered only when primary conf < 0.60. vs A4's 8 full backbones always-on.
↑ Fixes A4: 32.76M params overhead
LSTM Temporal Aggregator
Rolling window of 10 frames per face track. Stable clip-level label + confidence.
↑ Fixes A5: no temporal modeling
Intensity Ranking
Grad-CAM mean → MINIMAL / AVERAGE / STRONG. 3-panel PNG saved.
↑ Extends A3 ranking with Grad-CAM
Stream C — Quality

🔬 Technical Quality

Blur gate acts as hard veto. Blurry clips are eliminated regardless of emotion or object scores.

Q_i (blur) · M_i ∈ [0,1]
Blur Gate (Laplacian)
Q_i = Laplacian variance. If Q_i < θ_blur (100) → S_i = 0 regardless.
Hard veto: blurry ≠ highlight-worthy
Motion Energy
M_i = mean frame-difference magnitude. High for sports/action, low for static shots.
25% contribution to final S_i

10 Papers → Architectural Improvements

[A1] Gursesli 2024
FER confusion matrix — disgust/fear F1 ~40%
→ Binary expert CNNs for hard classes
[A2] Abbas 2025 (our primary paper)
CLCM — tested only on lab-controlled CK+
→ WeightedFERLoss (inverse-sqrt-freq)
[A3] Punuri 2024
LRP heatmaps fail on misclassified frames
→ Grad-CAM (works on ALL predictions)
[A4] Salman 2025
8 full MobileNetV2 experts (32.76M params)
→ 2 lightweight experts, triggered <60% conf only
[A5] Kosta 2023
No temporal modeling of emotion in video
→ LSTM rolling-window temporal aggregator
[B1] Hua 2025
Benchmark — always runs single heavy YOLO
→ Two-stage cascade (~69.5% FLOP savings)
[B2] Miri 2025
Flat confidence only, no track stability
→ Decomposed salience (conf+stability+persistence)
[B3] Shah 2026
Fixed τ=75 frames regardless of video fps
→ Adaptive τ = τ_sec × fps (normalised)
[B4] Yang 2024
IoU-only label assignment, ignores shape
→ SaIS = IoU + 0.5×shape_score (training-only)
[B5] Ramos 2025
Review only — no anchor-free inference validated
→ YOLOv8-S (anchor-free, decoupled head) Stage 1
